## Important corrections

- `12-22` previously stored Dongzhi as a permanent date even though the solstice can move. The record is now the transparent WhaDay prompt `Kış Sofrası Günü` / `Winter Table Day`.
- Knowledge Day and World Freedom Day now name their regional scope in the public title.
- World Population Day, World Mosquito Day and International Inuit Day use careful sharing and cannot enter engagement promotion.
- Identity, language and Indigenous copy centers first-person communities rather than treating them as curiosities.
