# Content curation batch 06 — January to June institutions

Date: 2026-08-14

## Outcome

- 60 remaining January-June civic, scientific, cultural and environmental records received bilingual editorial review.
- Curated coverage increased from 210 to 270 records; the queue fell to 96.
- Generic descriptions fell from 155 to 96 per locale.
- Default sharing hooks fell from 118 to 81 per locale.
- Verified source coverage increased from 120 to 178 records.

## Important corrections

- `04-15` was labeled “International Wellness Day,” but that fixed-date claim could not be supported. It is now the transparent WhaDay editorial prompt `İyi Hissetme Molası` / `Feel-Good Pause`.
- Four Chaplains Day is remembrance copy with shareability 1, not a playful viral day.
- UN Peacekeepers Day uses careful sharing and cannot enter engagement promotion.
- National and movement-origin observances use culture-specific provenance rather than being presented as universal UN days.

## Copy and classification

- Official observances now describe the practical reason the day exists instead of repeating the title.
- Recipient hooks connect teachers, engineers, artists, parents, seafarers and community workers to a real person-to-person action.
- Environmental copy distinguishes recycling from waste prevention and places food, energy and biodiversity in their wider systems.
